A newspaper-front-page infographic summing up the world news of
Wednesday, 18 November 2026, in a single 1024×1024 image.

**Stories covered**

- **Lead — Gulf on the brink:** US jets strike an Iranian missile site on
  Qeshm Island after Tehran's drones target a US radar base in Bahrain, as
  five ships are hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Ukraine:** Kyiv's drones and missiles bring down the road arch of the
  Kerch Bridge to Crimea; Moscow says it downed 1,441 drones in a second
  night of attacks.
- **Diplomacy:** APEC leaders meet in Shenzhen as Trump arrives from
  Manila, where the Quad met for the first time in two years; the first
  Trump–Xi–Putin summit follows the next day.
- **France:** 146,000 march in a public-sector strike over pay and the
  cost of living (unions put turnout above 500,000).
- **Russia:** 19 killed when a military An-12 transport crashes in the
  Kemerovo region.
- **Health:** US measles cases reach a 40-year high; the US, Mexico and
  Bolivia lose measles-free status.
- **Also:** a 5.3 quake shakes Romania; Voyager 1 nears one light-day
  from Earth.

Every headline, name and number was verified against at least two
reputable sources (Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Le Monde, IRNA) on the day.
The image is drawn entirely with PIL — no AI-generated text — so every
word stays crisp and legible.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the image to `/output/entry.png`. It uses only the Python
standard library, needs no network, and produces byte-identical output
on every run.
